# P4: same-seed verification of every legacy gated E4 run

Status: **closed, 14/14 bit-identical.**

Source of truth: [`results/e4_verify_all/COMPARE_ALL.md`](../e4_verify_all/COMPARE_ALL.md)
(generated by `code/experiments/compare_e4_verify_all.py`).
The earlier three-seed check is [`results/e4_fix/COMPARE.md`](../e4_fix/COMPARE.md)
(`code/experiments/compare_e4_fix.py`); it is superseded as the basis of the
claim and retained as the first three pairs.

## What was done

Every legacy gated E4 GPT-2 run that remains a reported result was re-executed
on its own seed under the corrected vector-valued Proposition-10 held bound

```
iota_t = Delta eta_t * Hbar_t + eta_max,t0 * (M_H * P_t + nu_H),
Delta eta_t = ||eta_t - eta_t0||_inf
```

that is, `dh.probe(rho, kappa, eta_vec=eta)` / `dh.bounds(eta)`, the default
path of `e4_gpt2_tta.py`. The shipped runs used the scalar interface
`dh.probe(rho, kappa)` / `dh.bounds(float(eta.max()))`, which forces
`Delta eta_t == 0` and reads `eta_max` at the current step rather than at the
last probe. That path survives behind `--legacy-hold`.

Driver flags were read back from the shipped JSONs and are identical per pair.

## Scope

14 runs, the complete legacy gated set:

| arm | r | order | seeds | pairs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| cohg_r0 | 0 | wiki,news,code | 0-7 | 8 |
| cohg | 4 | wiki,news,code | 0-2 | 3 |
| cohg_r0 | 0 | code,news,wiki | 0-2 | 3 |

The `fixed`, `hd` and `cohg_nogate` arms never consume `(rho_t, kappa_t)` and
are identical under either interface by construction, so they are excluded.
The mis-set initialization block and reverse-order seeds 3-7 were produced
under the corrected path and are not legacy.

## Outcome

All 14 pairs bit-identical: `gate_open_frac` and `coord_open_frac` equal, one
opening at step 1 in every run, max |d lambda| = 0 over `lam_hist`, per-step
loss traces bit-identical, delta online PPL = 0, `hvp_total` 4100/4100 for the
r=0 pairs and 4692/4692 for the r=4 pairs, `events` 0/0.

The shortcut was inert because lambda freezes after the single opening at
step 1, so `Delta eta_t = 0` for every t > 1 and `eta_max` at the current step
equals its value at the last probe. This is not a general equivalence of the
two interfaces.

## Execution

Local 2x RTX 3080, 2026-08-27 to 2026-08-29. Three runs were interrupted by
host-level process kills and restarted from scratch; only the final completed
runs are recorded above and in `COMPARE_ALL.md`.
